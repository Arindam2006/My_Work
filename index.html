GOOD TO KNOW / 08

A few things
you might be wondering.

Still have a question? Ask us directly ↗

How long does it take to build a website?

Timing depends on the size of your website and how quickly we can finalise content and feedback. We’ll share a clear timeline after understanding your project.

Do you build mobile-friendly websites?

Yes. Every website is designed to work across mobile phones, tablets and desktop screens.

Can I request a custom design?

Absolutely. We shape the design around your business, brand and audience rather than forcing a one-size-fits-all template.

Can I request changes?

Yes. We’ll agree on the project scope and feedback points together before development begins.

Do you provide website maintenance?

Maintenance needs vary. Let us know what ongoing support you’re looking for and we can discuss a suitable arrangement.

Can you integrate WhatsApp?

Yes. We can add a WhatsApp contact or enquiry link to make it easier for visitors to reach your business.

Can you add Google Maps?

Yes. A Google Maps location can be added to help customers find your business.

Can you build an e-commerce website?

Yes. We can discuss your products, catalogue and shopping requirements and plan a suitable e-commerce experience.
